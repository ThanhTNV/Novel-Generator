# Style Rules

- Use third-person limited POV unless otherwise specified.
- Show, don't tell — convey emotion through action, dialogue, and body language.
- Avoid adverb-heavy dialogue tags ("he said angrily"). Prefer strong verbs or action beats.
- Keep paragraphs varied in length. Avoid walls of text.
- Use dialogue to reveal character and advance plot, not to info-dump.
- Scene transitions should be smooth — use spatial, temporal, or emotional bridges.
- Avoid purple prose: vivid is good, overwrought is not.
- Each chapter should end on a hook or emotional beat that propels the reader forward.

# Magic System: The Draft Framework

## Overview
Magic in this world is not supernatural — it's structural. The world was literally designed by the Architects, and "magic" is the ability to perceive and manipulate the underlying framework (called the Draft). Think of it as accessing the source code of reality.

## How It Works
- **The Draft**: The invisible blueprint underlying all physical reality. Everything — stone, air, light, life — exists because the Draft defines it.
- **Draft-sight**: Rare individuals can perceive the Draft as overlaid patterns — geometric lines, luminous threads, architectural notation. Elara has latent Draft-sight inherited from her mother.
- **Manipulation**: Those with Draft-sight can make small edits — mending broken objects, altering material properties, bending light. Major edits require enormous concentration and risk destabilizing local reality.
- **Keystones**: Five fundamental anchor points in the Draft that hold the world's structure together. The Architects embedded them during creation. Removing them would allow wholesale revision of reality.

## Limitations
- Edits to the Draft are not free — they cause "draft fatigue," a physical and mental exhaustion that scales with the scope of the change.
- Edits near the Pale Threshold are unpredictable because the Draft is already damaged there.
- Living things resist editing. You can change stone easily; changing a person's body or mind is nearly impossible and morally abhorrent.
- The Sundering damaged the Draft itself, creating "null zones" where manipulation simply doesn't work.

## The Sundering
- An event ~200 years ago when the Architects attempted a major revision and something went wrong.
- The Draft partially collapsed in the eastern territories, creating the Ashenmere wasteland.
- Most Architects vanished or went dormant. The Pale Architect is the first to reappear.
- The Pale Threshold opened during the Sundering as a side effect — an unintended doorway.

## Key Rules
1. You cannot create something from nothing — only reshape what exists.
2. Draft-sight does not grant understanding — seeing the framework and comprehending it are different things.
3. The closer to a Keystone, the more rigid the Draft becomes. Edits near Keystones are nearly impossible.
4. The Pale Architect operates at a fundamentally higher level of Draft manipulation — comparable to an author versus a reader.

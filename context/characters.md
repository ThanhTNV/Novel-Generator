# Characters

## Elara Voss
- **Role**: Protagonist
- **Age**: 28
- **Appearance**: Tall and lean with sharp cheekbones. Dark auburn hair worn in a loose braid. Gray-green eyes flecked with gold. A thin scar runs along her left jawline from a childhood accident. Wears practical leather boots and a long charcoal coat.
- **Personality**: Fiercely independent, skeptical of authority, quick-witted but slow to trust. Masks vulnerability with dry humor. Deeply loyal once trust is earned.
- **Background**: Former archivist at the Athenaeum of Veiled Knowledge. Dismissed after discovering forbidden texts about the Sundering. Now works as an independent relic hunter.
- **Motivation**: Believes her missing brother Caelum is alive somewhere beyond the Pale Threshold. Will stop at nothing to find him.
- **Speech**: Clipped, precise sentences. Uses academic vocabulary naturally. Rarely raises her voice — gets quieter when angry.

## Theron Ashwick
- **Role**: Deuteragonist / love interest
- **Age**: 32
- **Appearance**: Broad-shouldered with warm brown skin and close-cropped black hair. Deep-set dark eyes with laugh lines. A brand mark on his right forearm — the sigil of the Ironveil Company. Dresses in well-worn traveling clothes, always with a belt knife.
- **Personality**: Charismatic and easygoing on the surface, but carries guilt over past actions as a mercenary. Pragmatic, street-smart, protective. Handles conflict with deflecting humor until pushed too far.
- **Background**: Former captain in the Ironveil Company, a mercenary guild. Left after a mission went wrong in Ashenmere. Now survives as a guide and smuggler along the border territories.
- **Motivation**: Seeks redemption for the people he failed to protect in Ashenmere. Drawn to Elara because she reminds him that conviction still exists.
- **Speech**: Warm, conversational tone. Uses idioms and colloquialisms. Tells stories to avoid direct questions about himself.

## The Pale Architect
- **Role**: Antagonist
- **Age**: Unknown (appears ageless)
- **Appearance**: Unnaturally symmetrical features. Alabaster skin that seems faintly luminous. Eyes like polished silver with no visible iris. Wears flowing white robes that appear woven from mist. Movements are fluid but unsettling — too smooth, too precise.
- **Personality**: Calm, patient, intellectually curious about mortals. Views humans as fascinating but ultimately expendable components of a greater pattern. Never raises voice. Cruelty expressed through indifference.
- **Background**: One of the Architects who designed the world's underlying magical framework. Went silent during the Sundering but has now returned with an agenda that requires unmaking parts of reality.
- **Motivation**: Believes the world is a failed draft that must be revised. Seeks the Five Keystones to initiate a Rewriting.
- **Speech**: Formal, archaic cadence. Uses metaphors of architecture, drafting, and revision. Never uses contractions.
